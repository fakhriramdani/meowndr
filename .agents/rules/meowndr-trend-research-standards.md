# Trend Research & Content Intelligence Standards (@meowndr)

These standards define mandatory research protocols, platform access rules, factual verification requirements, and content intelligence scoring for all short-form educational papercraft explainer reels produced for **`@meowndr`**.

## 1. Core Research Principles

1. **Research Trends Before Proposing New Episode Ideas**:
   - When trend research is requested or triggered on-demand, thorough empirical research must precede topic pitching.
   - Topics must be backed by verified audience demand signals, curiosity triggers, or emerging conversation volume.

2. **Geographic & Cultural Priority: Indonesia First**:
   - Prioritize Indonesian audience interests, cultural contexts, regional phenomena, and local curiosities.
   - Actively monitor global trends (English, Japanese, Latin American science/history communities) and assess their adaptability for Indonesian viewers with localized metaphors, relatable hooks, and culturally resonant papercraft visuals.

3. **Curated Niche Boundaries**:
   - Research topics must strictly focus on the @meowndr domain:
     - **Science**: Physics, Chemistry, Molecular Biology, Geology, Optics, Thermodynamics.
     - **Technology**: Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Photonics, Energy Systems.
     - **History**: Archaeology, Ancient Civilizations, Maritime History, Industrial Innovations, Pivotal Discoveries.
     - **Geography**: World Wonders, Extreme Biomes, Earth Curiosities, Geophysical Anomalies.
     - **Psychology**: Cognitive Biases, Perception Paradoxes, Behavioral Science, Memory Mechanisms.
     - **Nature & Animals**: Evolutionary Adaptations, Deep-Sea Biology, Biomimicry, Ecosystem Dynamics.
     - **Space & Cosmos**: Astrophysics, Orbital Mechanics, Planetary Science, Cosmic Mysteries.
     - **Mysteries & Interesting Facts**: Evidence-based unexplained phenomena, counter-intuitive paradoxes, natural wonders.

4. **Identify Emerging Topics Over Lifetime View Counts**:
   - Focus on growth momentum, search velocity, and rising audience engagement rather than historical evergreen videos with high cumulative lifetime views.
   - Distinguish between a genuinely growing new trend and an old viral video that has reached saturation.

5. **Strict Anti-Plagiarism & Creative Differentiation**:
   - **Never copy another creator's script, voiceover narration, visual assets, composition, or distinctive creative execution.**
   - Viral videos and trending queries serve exclusively as inspiration for underlying audience curiosity, not creative templates.
   - All scripts, storyboards, voiceovers, and Remotion papercraft scenes must be 100% original bespoke creations engineered from primary scientific/historical sources.

6. **Primary Source Fact Verification**:
   - Verify every scientific, technological, and historical claim against authoritative primary sources (peer-reviewed journals, institutional archives, academic bodies such as NASA, NOAA, BRIN, APS, Nature, Kemendikbud) before pitching it as an educational fact.
   - Zero tolerance for pseudoscience, unverified urban legends presented as facts, medical misinformation, or sensational clickbait without rigorous substance.

7. **Data Integrity & Metric Authenticity**:
   - Never fabricate engagement metrics, view counts, share numbers, or claim that a topic is trending without empirical evidence.
   - Distinguish strictly between measured metrics (directly obtained from official APIs) and estimated metrics (derived from search volume proxies or sample ratios).
   - If a metric is unavailable, record it explicitly as `UNAVAILABLE` or `null`.

8. **Source Traceability & Timestamping**:
   - Always record the exact research timestamp, source URL, channel/creator handle, platform origin, and publication date for every investigated topic.
   - Log all evidence in the episode research dossier and project source log.

9. **Respect Platform Terms & Compliance Boundaries**:
   - Adhere strictly to the official Developer Terms of Service, API rate limits, robots.txt directives, and privacy policies of YouTube (Google), TikTok (ByteDance), and Instagram (Meta).
   - Never bypass login requirements, CAPTCHAs, bot defenses, access controls, or platform restrictions.
   - Never utilize unauthorized scrapers, reverse-engineered private APIs, or credential interception.
   - When API access is unconfigured, utilize legitimate public discovery tools, search engines, or manual research fallbacks, and explicitly disclose all platform access limitations.

10. **Mandatory Speculative What-If Hypothesis & Multi-Era Cascade**:
   - All topics proposed for `@meowndr` must be engineered as speculative counterfactual scenarios ("Bagaimana Jika...") that explore profound downstream ripple effects across subsequent eras ("efeknya di masa selanjutnya").
   - Candidate topics must possess a plausible Point of Divergence (PoD) and demonstrate a clear 3-tier temporal cascade:
     1. *Immediate Shockwave*: Physical/geopolitical anomaly in the immediate aftermath.
     2. *Intermediate Era Domino*: Repercussions derailing decades, centuries, or evolutionary epochs.
     3. *Modern Civilization Consequence*: How our daily life, technology, or planetary state would look today.
   - Grounded in dual-layer factual evidence: real baseline constants verified alongside peer-reviewed cascade models.
   - Pure static trivia or flat descriptive recaps without counterfactual divergence are strictly retired.

---

## 2. Topic Rejection Criteria

Any topic meeting any of the following conditions must be immediately **REJECTED**:
- **Weak Factual Foundations**: Lack of verified peer-reviewed or authoritative documentation.
- **Brand Unsuitability**: Content containing gore, political partisanship, hate speech, vulgarity, or cynicism contrary to @meowndr's curious, optimistic, and educational tone.
- **Inaccurate Explanations**: Concepts that cannot be explained accurately and concisely within short-form video constraints (15–80 seconds) without introducing gross factual distortion.
- **Misleading / Sensational Claims**: Pseudoscience, paranormal hoaxes, clickbait medical claims, or fearmongering.
- **Zero Visual Storytelling Potential**: Purely abstract formulas or data tables that cannot be manifested through physical papercraft animations and Cat Scientist apparatuses.
- **Static Trivia / Zero Temporal Cascade**: Flat facts, static listicles, or isolated trivia events lacking a speculative Point of Divergence or downstream ripple effects into subsequent eras.

---

## 3. Human Approval Sovereignty

- Trend research informs topic selection; it never automatically dictates creative direction or overrides human editorial judgement.
- The creator/user retains absolute final authority at Gate 1 (Topic & Hook Approval), Gate 2 (Script Approval), and Gate 3 (Final Video Master Approval).
