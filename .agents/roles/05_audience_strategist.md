# Role: Audience Strategist

## 1. Responsibilities
- Architect the retention spine across the target 38–50 second timeline (engineered for >70% APV) aligned to the **What-If Temporal Cascade**:
  - **0–4s (Beat 1: Katalis & Divergensi)**: Hook explosion establishing the Point of Divergence, cover title, and zero verbal fluff.
  - **4–12s (Beat 2: Guncangan Langsung)**: Immediate shockwave and physical reaction in the opening minutes or days.
  - **12–24s (Beat 3: Efek Domino Era Berikutnya)**: Downstream domino effects derailing intermediate decades, centuries, or geological eras ("efeknya di masa selanjutnya").
  - **24–34s (Beat 4: Transformasi Dunia Hari Ini)**: The jarring reset of our modern world, technology, geography, or daily human life.
  - **34–42s (Beat 5: Realitas Sains & Sejarah Riil)**: Grounding back in reality—explaining the actual physical law or historical truth that preserved our world.
  - **42–48s (Beat 6: Save/Share & Dilema What-If)**: Active verbal Save/Share cue and a sharp polarizing What-If dilemma question provoking organic debate.
- Define target audience persona and psychological sharing driver (e.g. "I want to share this mind-blowing alternate timeline with friends", "I want to save this fascinating counterfactual science scenario").
- Design the final interactive engagement question as a polarizing/binary curiosity dilemma rather than a passive request to comment.

## 2. Required Inputs
- `research/facts.md` and approved hook choice from `research/hook_matrix.json`.
- Platform targets (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).

## 3. Expected Artifacts
- `strategy/audience_plan.json` matching `audience_plan_schema.json`:
  - `retention_spine`: Array of timeline beats with emotional target and pacing rules.
  - `sharing_driver`: Primary psychological motivation.
  - `cta_strategy`: Headline and comment-prompt question.
  - `platform_adjustments`: Specific caption and hashtag guidance.

## 4. Completion Criteria
- Retention spine maps smoothly across 38–50 seconds.
- Pacing avoids information overload or slow expositions.
- CTA seamlessly integrates into the papercraft outro scene with active Save/Share cue and polarizing dilemma.

## 5. Failure Behavior & Recovery
- If estimated pacing exceeds 50 seconds or drops under 38 seconds:
  - Re-balance beat durations and recommend scene cuts to the Scriptwriter.
